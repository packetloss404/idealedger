# Autonomous Research Synthesis — 2026-08-10

**Scope:** 273 canonical ideas and four autonomous Round-10 loops, plus the 2026-08-10 simulated focus-group synthesis.

**Official category framing:** The hackathon spans five categories—**Education & Human Potential**, **Entrepreneurship & Job Creation**, **Small Business Services**, **Money & Financial Access**, and **Professional Services Access**. Their stated themes are respectively learning and human growth; founder and economy-building tools; everyday business competitiveness; access to banking, capital, and financial freedom; and access to expert guidance.

**Method:** Three independent teams screened consumer anger, irreversible moments, and willingness to pay, then attacked each other's leads. Across Loop 1 through Loop 4 they evaluated 27 + 29 + 29 + 38 = **123 serious candidates**. The final synthesis separately ranked commercial quality, technical authority, and cheapest decisive experiment.

## Judging criteria and candidate ranking

The supplied judging criteria are the decisive screen: **Business Viability** requires a launched business with real users, real revenue, and a sustainable model; **AI-Native Operations** requires AI live in production executing key decisions; and **Category Impact** requires a fundamental workflow shift or credible widespread adoption. The current work funds validation experiments, not winners. LotMatch is the best overall finalist candidate, WeedCheck is the strongest AI-visible alternate, CaptionGap is conditional accessibility research, DoorHold is a useful business test but weak on AI-native operations, CrashTape is an engineering reserve, and DeferralGap is concierge-only.

No candidate has yet met all three criteria. A submission must show unaffiliated paying customers, production decision logs, abstention and human-review paths, model and cost telemetry, repeat use, and measurable category outcomes—not merely a demo or simulated focus-group enthusiasm.


Stop broad mining for now. The Ledger contains enough technically plausible ideas; the scarce evidence is paid behavior, live adherence, and zero-false-green performance.

No product is approved for a production build. Fund five experiments only:

1. **LotMatch** — strongest business probability.
2. **WeedCheck** — strongest visual and hackathon experiment.
3. **DoorHold** — cheapest no-code business test.
4. **CaptionGap** — compensated DHH/HoH co-design plus a separate paid-demand test.
5. **DeferralGap** — paid concierge reconciliation only.

Keep **CrashTape** as an engineering reserve, not a funded commercial bet. Keep **Confirmed Spoken Details** as mission-led accessibility research. **CancelBy** remains a cheap alternate test, but the native Calendar/Reminders substitute keeps it out of the funded five.

Only five of 273 records—1.8 percent—clear the commercial experiment bar. That selectivity is a feature, not a failure.

## Where the three teams agreed and disagreed

All three teams ranked LotMatch and WeedCheck in the top tier. Technical review also favored DoorHold's owned clock and response event, while commercial review favored its business payer and no-install client flow. CaptionGap survived because no exact pipeline-health indicator surfaced and paid caption products establish nearby willingness to pay, but every reviewer bounded it to an app-owned session.

The disagreement is useful:

- Consumer and technical reviewers rank CrashTape highly because its kill-and-recover demo tests difficult, app-owned truth. Commercial review demotes it because recovery must remain free and paid recorder features are commodity.
- Consumer review keeps CancelBy as a four-day alternate test. Commercial review would stop it because a configured recurring Reminder solves the core job.
- Technical review scores several API-clean ideas highly, including Todoist Reset and Loop, but commercial review excludes them because recurrence, substitution, and payer proof remain weak.

The portfolio below therefore separates commercial funding from engineering and mission reserves.

## LotMatch

**Offer:** refundable $39.99 founder-year to flooring businesses.

**Why it survives:** business payer, repeat jobs, a natural check before irreversible installation, and visible product, lot, shade, or caliber tokens. The risk is not only OCR; the owner pays while a hurried crew performs the ritual.

**Experiment:** collect 300 dual-transcribed labels across ten brands, including matches, seeded single-field differences, and difficult or unreadable labels. Then test ten crews across five live jobs.

**Go only if:**

- accepted-token accuracy exceeds 99 percent;
- there are zero false `MATCH` results;
- every uncertain field becomes `UNREADABLE`;
- median scan time is under four seconds;
- crews scan more than 90 percent of cartons; and
- at least three of ten flooring-business owners prepay.

**Immediate stop:** any false match, more than ten percent of target cartons lacking usable lot fields, crew adherence below 90 percent, or a payer requirement for dealer/ERP integration.

**Budget and time:** up to $1,500 and 21 elapsed days. Collect fixtures before building a native UI.

See [the original LotMatch research](IDEA-MINING-LOOP-2026-08-09.md#1-lotmatch--strongest-business-candidate).

## WeedCheck

**Offer:** refundable $29.99 annual production pass to active paid-garment sellers.

**Why it survives:** an extremely legible before/after demo at the moment before a destructive heat press. Its weakness is scope: glare, glitter, fine lettering, wrinkles, and multi-color work may shrink the eligible market.

**Experiment:** prepare 100 opaque, non-glitter HTV pieces on a four-fiducial mat—50 correct and 50 containing one to three seeded errors over 3 mm. Only after the fixture passes should 20 sellers test the assisted checker on real paid orders.

**Go only if:**

- seeded-error recall is at least 95 percent;
- false holds stay below five percent;
- median result time is under ten seconds;
- routine manual alignment is unnecessary;
- at least 15 sellers check more than 70 percent of eligible paid orders; and
- at least five of 20 sellers prepay.

**Immediate stop:** fewer than five prepays, an eligible scope below half of ordinary orders, manual alignment above 20 percent, or voluntary use below 70 percent.

**Budget and time:** up to $1,600 and 24 elapsed days. Build the fixture and algorithm before the app shell.

See [the original WeedCheck research](IDEA-MINING-LOOP-2026-08-09.md#2-weedcheck--strongest-shipaton-candidate).

## DoorHold

**Offer:** refundable $24.99 annual plan to solo mobile-service providers.

**Why it survives:** the app can own the grace clock and a no-install capability-link response at an already monetized lockout. It may claim only that the link holder selected an option—never identity, physical access, payment, or legal acceptance.

**Experiment:** use signed web links, manual SMS, and a lightweight response page with 20 solo providers and 40 staged or live no-access incidents. Do not build a native app.

**Go only if:**

- real lockout incidence is at least one per 100 appointments;
- providers use the flow on at least 32 of 40 staged incidents;
- at least 24 of 40 clients respond within the grace window;
- median response is under five minutes; and
- at least four providers prepay.

**Immediate stop:** calls or ordinary SMS consistently outperform the link, incidence is too low, most targets already use a field-service suite, or support costs break the price.

**Budget and time:** up to $400, two to three setup days, and a one-week study.

See [the DoorHold research](IDEA-MINING-LOOP-2026-08-09-ROUND5.md#doorhold).

## CaptionGap

**Offer:** after compensated co-design freezes the prototype and copy, a separate non-compensated buyer cohort sees the demo and may place a refundable $7.99 preorder.

**Why it survives:** paid caption products prove nearby spending and no exact app-owned pipeline-health indicator surfaced. The app cannot inspect Apple Live Captions or infer omitted speech.

**Experiment:** 12 fairly compensated DHH or hard-of-hearing co-design participants use an instrumented app-owned session for at least ten real hours. Inject 100 buffer, interruption, model, and recognizer failures. Then recruit a different cohort of 12 qualified target buyers who receive no study compensation, show them the frozen product plus native/free alternatives, and ask for a refundable preorder.

**Go only if:**

- warnings stay under one false alert per real hour;
- all 100 injected failures produce zero false-healthy states;
- at least eight users say the indicator changed their response; and
- at least four of the 12 separate, non-compensated buyers place a refundable preorder.

**Claim boundary:** only `audio observed`, `recognizer updating`, `interrupted`, or `no recent result`. Never claim complete captions or semantic omission.

**Immediate stop:** any false healthy state, fewer than four separate-buyer prepays, increased anxiety without agency, or clear preference for native/free caption tools.

**Budget and time:** up to $2,200 and 21 elapsed days, including fair participant compensation and DHH/HoH facilitation.

See [the Loop-4 CaptionGap decision](IDEA-MINING-LOOP-2026-08-10-ROUND10-LOOP4.md#captiongap).

## DeferralGap

**Offer:** charge $7.99 before a human-assisted retirement-contribution reconciliation.

**Why it survives:** the consequence is serious and no exact consumer matcher surfaced. It remains only a concierge test because settlement delay, aggregation, reversals, corrections, and employer-match cadence make simplistic equality unsafe.

**Experiment:** recruit 30 people actively searching for missing or confusing elective-deferral contributions. Compare user-supplied payroll deductions with visible plan history using only `located`, `not yet locatable`, or `needs review`.

**Go only if:**

- all 30 actively concerned employees pay $7.99;
- at least 20 share both artifacts in under 90 seconds and repeat monthly for three months;
- 100 real pairs produce zero cases where `not yet locatable` appears while a supported match is already visible; and
- three persistent review cases receive employer or provider confirmation.

**Immediate stop:** users do not repeat, support or privacy work exceeds ten minutes per customer, acquisition costs exceed roughly $4 at the $7.99 price, or timing ambiguity prevents a low-false-hold model.

See [the Loop-2 DeferralGap decision](IDEA-MINING-LOOP-2026-08-10-ROUND10-LOOP2.md#deferralgap).

**Budget and time:** up to $1,000 in direct recruitment and participant costs; the repeat-behavior gate runs for three months.

## CrashTape

CrashTape is the strongest engineering reserve and the best kill-and-recover demonstration, but not currently one of the five commercial experiments. The recovery and truthful custody layer must remain free; folders, formats, and export features face mature recorder competition.

If engineering capacity is available, run only the durability spike: 100 fixtures across force termination, lock/background, calls and Siri, route changes, low storage, pressure, and long sessions on three current iPhones. Pass requires 100/100 committed segments reopening, no duplicated or reordered buffers, zero false `Verified` states, no more than two seconds of terminal loss, and inaudible boundaries. Promotion also requires at least 10 of 20 field interviewers to choose it as their primary recorder for two weeks. Stop before library or paywall work on any technical failure.

Commercial resurrection requires target interviewers to pay specifically for premium organization or export features—not merely praise free recovery.

See [the Round-8 CrashTape research](IDEA-MINING-LOOP-2026-08-09-ROUND8.md#conditional-survivor-crashtape).

## Confirmed Spoken Details

Confirmed Spoken Details remains the mission-led accessibility alternate. Speaker confirmation converts fallible transcription into a bounded human acknowledgment, but passing the phone adds ceremony and the confirmation core should not be forced behind a consumer paywall.

Continue only through DHH-led co-design with 8–12 participants: at least 90 percent usable token capture, median focused-card latency below 1.2 seconds, no confirmed record without a physical confirm/edit action, and week-two reuse. Commercial resurrection requires either three institutions prepaying at least $500 for a pilot or four of 12 individuals independently paying after demonstrated reuse.

See [the Confirmed Spoken Details research](IDEA-MINING-LOOP-2026-08-08.md#2-confirmed-spoken-details--conditional-co-design-test).

## CancelBy

CancelBy is not funded in the five-experiment portfolio. Its computed deadline is honest when the user-entered policy is current, but the app cannot know whether policy changed or cancellation succeeded, and a configured recurring Calendar/Reminder flow is a brutal free substitute.

If tested at all, keep it no-code and capped at four days and $350: 20 weekly therapy, PT, or lesson users; at least 30 percent with a late fee in the prior year; at least half preferring the money-at-risk card in a blind comparison against a configured 49-hour Reminder; 80 percent setup under one minute; and four $14.99 lifetime prepurchases. Do not lower the price to manufacture conversion.

## Ten-business-day execution order

Run recruitment in parallel but fund engineering sequentially:

1. Sell LotMatch founder-years to ten flooring businesses and collect real labels.
2. Show WeedCheck's constrained fixture to 20 garment sellers and charge before releasing an assisted checker.
3. Run DoorHold entirely through signed web links and staged messages.
4. Recruit CaptionGap co-designers through DHH/HoH partners, test action value and injected failures, then run the separate buyer-cohort preorder test.
5. Sell DeferralGap concierge analyses through high-intent search or communities.

Only one code-heavy candidate may advance. Rank passing experiments by:

`collected prepayment × eligible-event frequency × observed adherence ÷ support burden`

Maximum suggested direct spend if all five run is $6,700: $1,500 LotMatch, $1,600 WeedCheck, $400 DoorHold, $2,200 CaptionGap, and $1,000 DeferralGap. One failed mandatory gate means no build.

## Durable rules from 123 screens

1. **No owned or sanctioned state, no product.** Pain inside another app does not grant access to its truth.
2. **Authority is necessary but insufficient.** Exact bytes or callbacks still lose when a cheaper focused utility owns the same action.
3. **Acknowledgment proves only the acknowledgment.** It does not prove identity, custody, payment, waking, feeding, completion, or safety.
4. **Unknown must be a first-class output.** Use `unreadable`, `not comparable`, `not yet locatable`, and `unavailable`; never turn uncertainty green.
5. **A false green is an immediate kill.** Last-inch gates require zero false matches in their supported fixture set.
6. **Two records need a stable invariant.** Different timing, aggregation, identifiers, and physical boundaries make arithmetic comparison non-authoritative.
7. **A new ritual must prove adherence before engineering.** Replacing Camera, Wallet, clipboard, captions, calls, or a host portal is commercially brutal.
8. **The host owning correction makes the phone checker a feature.** File facts are not a product when the DAW, slicer, uploader, or provider portal owns the fix.
9. **Fixture before interface.** Collect 100–300 real edge cases before polishing camera, speech, recovery, or reconciliation UI.
10. **A striking demo is not recurring demand.** Require repeated use and collected payment after showing the native or free substitute.
11. **Trust-critical recovery and accessibility fundamentals should not be paywalled.** Monetize organization, scale, collaboration, or services around them.
12. **The Ledger has reached experiment saturation.** New broad mining now has lower expected value than these sharply priced tests.

## Final decision

Fund LotMatch, WeedCheck, and DoorHold as the primary commercial-validation portfolio. Run CaptionGap as compensated co-design followed by an independent paid-demand test, and DeferralGap only as paid concierge learning. Keep CrashTape as an optional technical reserve and Confirmed Spoken Details as mission-led research. Do not begin a production build until one experiment clears payment, repeated behavior, authority, and distribution gates simultaneously.
