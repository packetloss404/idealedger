## The 10 most important lessons Round 11 must inherit

1. **This is a revenue-and-growth contest, not a concept contest.** Grand Prize = RevenueCat revenue shortlist + sustainable growth story. Round 11 must rank ideas by *speed to paying users*, not cleverness. The 2-minute demo is table stakes.
2. **Businesses pay faster than consumers** (evidenced by LotMatch/WeedCheck/DoorHold prepay logic). For Shipaton's 6-week revenue window, a payer who buys within days beats a consumer who might buy in month 6.
3. **Do not re-enter two-party adoption / cold-start territory.** No marketplace, no "both sides must install," no network required before value.
4. **Do not re-derive the commodity patterns** listed above; they are already represented in the Ledger. New work must clear a higher bar of differentiation.
5. **Authority/truth rules still apply to the Shipaton track.** Products that imply certainty they lack (safety, legality, eligibility, authenticity) are non-starters regardless of revenue appeal.
6. **The phone-native advantage must be real**, not decorative. If a website is equally good, we are wasting Shipaton's strongest lever (and 7 weeks).
7. **Retention is a category, not an afterthought.** OneSignal's Keep Them Coming Back is the top non-Grand prize ($25K); a product without a natural weekly ritual forfeits it.
8. **Monetization must be designed in** (HAMM award: well-crafted paywall, thoughtful pricing). Free-then-throwaway apps cannot win the awards that pay the most.
9. **Real external evidence beats synthetic enthusiasm.** Every finalist needs public evidence of the pain, current behavior, and willingness to pay — not simulated focus-group quotes.
10. **The idea must be shippable and store-publishable in ~6 weeks**, US-accessible, new app, passing App Review. This eliminates hardware, regulated judgment, and heavy backends.

## Round-11 mission

Discover a Shipaton-grade opportunity that can (a) be released as a brand-new app in the US App Store within ~51 days, (b) produce real paying users and revenue in the window, (c) fit a natural retention loop, (d) monetize natively via RevenueCat (subscription/IAP/ads), and (e) survive the truth/authority and anti-commodity gates. Zero survivors is an acceptable outcome; a false winner is not.
