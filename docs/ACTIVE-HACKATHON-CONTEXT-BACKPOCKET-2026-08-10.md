# Active Hackathon Context — BackPocket.help — 2026-08-10

## Purpose

The Idea Ledger is a durable, cross-hackathon think tank and decision archive. It is **not** the strategy database for one contest. Ideas, rejected concepts, research dossiers, validation gates, and postmortems should remain reusable across future hackathons, products, and research rounds.

## Current active build

**BackPocket.help** is the product currently being built for the **Build with Gemini XPRIZE**:

- Hackathon: https://xprize.devpost.com/
- Product: https://backpocket.help
- Official deadline: **August 17, 2026 at 1:00pm PDT**
- Time remaining at the August 10 verification point: **about one week**

The live product is an **AI-operated, human-finished local rescue-service prototype**. A customer shows what is broken, jammed, confusing, or physically difficult. Four bounded agent roles scope the job and safety boundary, reason about constraints and a Plan B, prepare fulfillment, and model the business. A human rescuer then verifies, authorizes, and finishes the supported physical work.

The current site demonstrates a dresser-drawer rescue and a **$69 on-site quick-rescue offer** with a 60-minute service window and a 20-minute service area. It also states an explicit exclusion boundary around gas, mains electricity, refrigerants, structural work, major plumbing, vehicle safety systems, and similar regulated or high-risk jobs.

**Current evidence boundary:** the site labels the rescue as a synthetic demo. It does not claim a real address, payment, booking, dispatched worker, unaffiliated customer, revenue, or completed field job. Those are the next facts the business must establish; the polished demo is not itself validation.

## XPRIZE-specific constraints

The team is optimizing BackPocket.help for the XPRIZE hackathon. The relevant questions are:

1. Can BackPocket.help acquire unaffiliated customers and complete paid supported jobs before the deadline?
2. Is AI live in production and executing key business decisions rather than decorating the product?
3. Does the current small-job rescue loop fit Small Business Services or Professional Services Access strongly enough to demonstrate category impact?
4. Can the team prove safe triage, fulfillment reliability, positive contribution margin, and a repeatable acquisition path?
5. What evidence belongs in the product telemetry, customer records, revenue records, demo, and Devpost submission?

The exact current XPRIZE rules and dates remain authoritative at the Devpost URL. This dossier records planning context supplied by the team; it is not a substitute for the official rules.

## Ledger policy

- Keep current hackathon context in dossiers and project records, not in every idea's pitch.
- Tag ideas and research by opportunity when a schema extension supports it; do not rewrite history to make one hackathon the organizing principle.
- Preserve rejected ideas and their resurrection triggers across contests.
- Record real users, revenue, retention, and experiments separately from simulated persona research.
- When a hackathon ends, archive its outcome as a postmortem and leave the general corpus intact.

## Immediate BackPocket research questions

- Which narrow supported job can be acquired and fulfilled repeatedly without crossing the published safety boundary?
- Will an unaffiliated customer pay the displayed $69 price before meeting the rescuer?
- How often do the agents abstain or escalate, and how often does the human override the proposed scope, safety decision, parts, or Plan B?
- What are travel time, hands-on time, refund risk, support cost, model cost, and contribution margin per completed job?
- Which acquisition channel reaches a customer at the moment “the normal way is not working” without marketplace cold start?
- Can the submission show real customer consent, payment, agent decision logs, fulfillment evidence, and an honest P&L by the August 17 deadline?
