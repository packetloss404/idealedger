## Eligibility

- Open to individuals at age of majority, teams, and organizations. (Devpost UI also says "Ages 13 to 99 only" for the register page.)
- Excluded: OFAC-sanctioned countries/regions (Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, etc.); Promotion Entities and their agents/family; judges and their employers.
- No purchase or payment necessary to enter or win.
- One entrant may submit multiple distinct submissions.

## Platform & publishing requirements

- Platforms: iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or Android (Devpost UI also lists Samsung Galaxy Store as a launch surface, new this year).
- **The first public version of the app must be released during the Submission Period (Aug 1 – Sep 30, 2026)** on Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or Samsung Galaxy Store. Updates to previously released apps do NOT qualify. Brainstorming/building/posting before Aug 1 is allowed; first release must be inside the window.
- **All apps must be accessible from the United States.**
- Must integrate the **RevenueCat SDK** to power at least one in-app or web purchase, **or** serve ads through **RevenueCat Ads**.
- Must offer a **free trial** OR provide a **promo code** so judges can unlock the IAP and test premium features.

## Submission materials

- Text description of features/functionality.
- **Demo video ≤ 2 minutes** (judges not required to watch beyond 2 min), showing app running on its device, public on YouTube or Vimeo, no third-party trademarks/copyrighted music.
- URL to fully published app on App Store / Google Play / Galaxy Store (Next Gen exempt).
- 1024×1024 app icon.
- At least one screenshot at **1179×2556 px with no device frame**.
- Free trial or promo code (Next Gen exempt).
- All materials in English or translated.

- ~$685,000+ cash (marketing copy says over $700K / over $1M total value incl. prizes).

## Categories and judging criteria (what the competition actually rewards)

1. **Grand Prize — $100,000** (top prize; includes NYC travel + Times Square billboard + 9to5Mac/9to5Google spotlight).
   - **Shortlisting:** total revenue generated during the Submission Period as reported in RevenueCat. Highest revenue does not auto-win.
   - **Judging:** quality, efficiency, sustainability of growth that produced the revenue. Criteria: Early and Effective Release; Growth by numbers (installs, active users, paying customers, conversion, retention, MRR/ARR, waitlist, social reach, community engagement).
2. **#BuildInPublic Award — $30K/$20K/$10K.** Sharing story, engagement, lessons learned incorporated from public feedback. Audience size does NOT matter.
3. **HAMM Award (Help Apps Make Money) — $15K/$10K/$5K.** Most robust and creative monetization strategy: well-crafted paywall, thoughtful pricing/packaging, conversion, monetization that genuinely fits, innovative/diverse revenue mix, sustainable model beyond the hackathon.
4. **RevenueCat Design Award — $15K/$10K/$5K.** Craft separate from business potential: innovative technology/designs, aesthetics, animations, gestures, delightful art.
5. **RevenueCat Peace Prize — $15K/$10K/$5K.** Greatest social good: impact on individuals/communities/society; feasibility and potential reach.
6. **Best Game Award — $15K/$10K/$5K.** Fun/engaging, unique gameplay/progression/replayability, monetization fit for genre.
7. **Catvertising Award — $15K/$10K/$5K.** Creative and effective use of **RevenueCat Ads**: natural/additive placements, thoughtful integration with the rest of the revenue stack, users don't hate it.
8. **Next Gen Award — $15K/$10K/$5K.** Student-only; judged on video + public open-source repo; no store release or paid developer account needed.
9. **Conflict of Interest Award.** RevenueCat/sponsor employees only; no cash.

## Strategic implications captured for Round 11

- The **Grand Prize is fundamentally a growth-and-revenue contest**: shortlist = RevenueCat-reported revenue during the window; winner = most credible, sustainable growth story. Idea quality matters only insofar as it produces traction. The two-minute demo is a submission floor, not the win condition.
- The highest-value non-Grand prizes reward **retention tooling (OneSignal $25K), monetization craft (HAMM), and revenue momentum (Replit Idea-to-Income, Stripe web volume)**.
- App must be new, released in the window, US-accessible, and monetized via RevenueCat. This means: no marketplace/network cold-start, no hardware dependency, no regulated professional judgment, must pass App Review in ~6 weeks.
- A product with (a) a business or prosumer payer who pays within days, (b) a natural weekly ritual, (c) a shareable result, and (d) a crafted freemium/paywall could contend for Grand + HAMM + OneSignal + Layers + Replit simultaneously.
- The Christopher Lawley Productivity influencer category is a separate save-and-retrieve opportunity; do not use the stale read-later description of BackPocket.help when judging it.

## Monetization Canary contest relevance

Monetization Canary is the Round 11 candidate most directly aligned with HAMM: its proposed unit is factual offering, price-data, paywall-attempt, and purchase-result health by build and storefront. That contest alignment is not validation. It still fails if ordinary observability reproduces the diagnosis quickly, developers reject another probe, or RevenueCat can add the alert directly.
