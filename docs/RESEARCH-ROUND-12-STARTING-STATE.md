## Why Round 12 Build-Out exists

Broad mining is exhausted (two independent rounds agree). Round 12 Build-Out pivots from discovery to **build-out and sharpening**, plus the protocol stages that Round 12 compressed:

1. **Weirdness Round** (mandated, under-executed in R11): 20 strongest researched pains × unusual-solver lenses → ~30 divergent ideas → same evidence gates.
2. **Loser cross-pollination**: extract reusable mechanisms from ~120 dead concepts; find valuable mechanisms hidden inside failed products.
3. **Process-tracker deep-dive**: the C11 "what happens next" cluster had the most convergence (7 research paths) but R11 killed the gov-silo version on the no-sanctioned-API rule. The *user-instrumentable* version (user logs their own steps and evidence across any multi-provider process) was never properly investigated. This is the round's primary new-discovery bet.
4. **Finalist killer-check + falsification design**: re-audit SnapInvoice for any missed competitor; design the cheapest decisive experiments precisely (fixture, recruitment, prepay).
5. **Six-week build-out**: for the top candidate, produce an actual launch plan (MVP scope, architecture, RevenueCat monetization design, 2-minute demo storyboard, Aug 11 → Sep 30 calendar, acquisition playbook, Grand-Prize revenue math, pre-mortem).
