## Discoveries

### Wave 2 — Independent lane synthesis

- The library/academic lane opened full text for death-registration history, EDRS voice signatures, death-certificate audits, CRVS integration, NIST exception handling, FAA repair records, and OMB corrective-action controls. It found a durable mechanism family, not a validated funeral market.
- The pain/service lane opened 26 pages across funeral, cottage manufacturing, rental, laboratory, and hazardous waste. Rental handoffs and sample acceptance have the strongest repeated physical events; hazardous manifests have the strongest official digitization and host ownership.
- The technology lane opened current Apple, Google, Expo, RevenueCat, and agent-tool documentation. Capability changes lower inference, integration, and payment friction; they do not establish demand or authority.
- The competitor/graveyard lane opened 50+ funeral and cemetery sources plus current product surfaces. FuneralOS, CaseNX, FuneralHQ, and incumbents weaken the prior claim that the funeral queue is empty. The right remaining question is whether a neutral exception layer is worth paying for.

### Wave 3 — Independent second checks

- **Equipment rental:** kill a generic condition-evidence app. Point of Rental, Record360, Quipli, Booqable, EZRentOut, Trackunit, Cheqroom, and generic inspection platforms occupy the workflow. A paid `Return Evidence Desk` for high-value serial-numbered equipment remains a human concierge experiment only.
- **Lab sample acceptance:** repeated rejection/qualification work is measurable, but LabWare, CrelioHealth, STARLIMS, LabVantage, and LIMS modules are direct/host-absorbable. A one-week single-lab pilot is the only acceptable next step.
- **Customs:** ACE/CATAIR/ISF/PSC materials show precise deadlines and authority boundaries. KlearNow, CargoWise, Magaya, Descartes, e2open, and broker services already cover the feature surface. A broker-owned document-chase shadow pilot is a possible research test, not a standalone product.

### Wave 4 — Technology and authority correction

- Apple Foundation Models now exposes image understanding, structured output, tool calling, and Private Cloud Compute; Google ML Kit GenAI exposes local Gemini Nano APIs on a constrained device list with foreground and quota limits.
- Expo SDK 57 widgets/Live Activities are available through development builds; RevenueCat Web supports web purchases and shared entitlements but documents a B2B limitation for RevenueCat Billing.
- QR/NFC/UWB identify or locate a device-relative artifact; they do not prove identity, possession, causality, safety, or acceptance. `FieldProof Ledger` is therefore a feature/integration hypothesis, not a new company.

## Contradictions

1. Customs is the most repeatable economic workflow in the bootstrapper view, but current platforms already sell the exact document/exceptions surface. Resolution: one broker-paid shadow pilot; no build.
2. Funeral research found a historically persistent certifier chase, while current funeral PMS products expose missing/pending document queues. Resolution: separate workflow existence from an unoccupied product wedge.
3. Rental vendors report large damage recovery, while their own app reviews show upload and camera reliability failures. Resolution: measure recovered dollars and accepted signatures, not report generation.
4. On-device inference is now cheap, but device coverage and quotas are narrow. Resolution: benchmark supported devices and keep explicit human review/fallback.
5. A digital government workflow can be authoritative while still being inaccessible to a neutral sidecar. Resolution: product boundary stops at preparation, routing, and audit; acceptance remains with the host.
